In the 2030s, humanity could ignite on Earth the same energy that makes the stars shine.

It's called fusion: two light atomic nuclei join together and release an enormous amount of energy, with almost no emissions and with a fuel that comes from water. For decades it was a laboratory experiment. Today it is a race among companies and countries to build the first power plant that brings that electricity to our homes.

Every week, an article to understand this revolution: its history, the politics behind it, the industry building it and what it costs.
